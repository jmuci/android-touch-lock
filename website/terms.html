What Touch Lock does

Touch Lock is a parenting-control utility for Android, downloaded and configured by a parent or guardian, that displays a touch-blocking overlay so a device can be handed to a young child or used hands-free (for example, during a video call) without accidental taps. An optional add-on, Toddler-Proof Lock, uses an Android Accessibility service to reinforce the lock. Full details on how the app works and what data it accesses are in the Privacy Policy.

Intended use — supervision required

Touch Lock reduces the chance of accidental touches; it does not replace an adult's judgment or attention. Specifically:

  • No lock mechanism — including Toddler-Proof Lock — can guarantee that every tap, swipe, or hardware button (volume, power) is blocked on every device and Android version.
  • Touch Lock does not restrict phone calls, emergency dialing, or any function Android itself always keeps available.
  • Touch Lock is not a childproofing, safety, or medical device, and must not be relied on as the sole means of supervising a child's use of a device.
  • Always keep a child within your sight and hearing while a device is locked with Touch Lock, the same as you would with any screen you hand to them.
