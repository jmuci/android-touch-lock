Touch Lock — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Summary: Touch Lock works entirely offline. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or analytics. There are no user accounts, no ads, and no third-party SDKs of any kind.

What Touch Lock is

Touch Lock is a parenting-control utility for Android. It's downloaded, installed, and configured by a parent or guardian to temporarily block touch input on a device — for example, handing a phone to a young child to watch a video, or preventing accidental taps during a video call — while keeping whatever's on screen fully visible.

Permissions and what they're for

  • Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW) — required to draw the touch-blocking overlay on top of whatever app is open. The overlay is rendered entirely on-device; nothing about the app underneath it is read, recorded, or transmitted.
  • Foreground service — keeps the lock active and shows a persistent notification while Touch Lock is running, as Android requires for any app doing ongoing background work.
  • Post notifications — required to show the lock/unlock notification.
  • Vibrate — haptic feedback on lock/unlock. No data involved.
  • Accessibility service (optional — "Toddler-Proof Lock") — off by default, and only active if a parent explicitly enables it via a dedicated in-app disclosure screen. While active, it receives window-change events containing only the package name of the app currently in the foreground (for example, com.google.android.youtube) — never text, images, or any other on-screen content. This is used solely to detect when the device has navigated away from the protected app while locked, so Touch Lock can bring it back to the front, to block the Back button while locked, and to close the notification shade if it's pulled down while locked. This information is processed entirely on-device in real time, is never stored beyond the current lock session, never transmitted off-device, and never shared with any third party. This service is not a tool for users with disabilities and is not declared as one.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, files, browsing history, or any analytics or usage statistics. The app makes no network requests of any kind — Touch Lock has no server, no backend, and no internet permission to use even if it wanted to.

Data stored on your device

Touch Lock stores a small amount of local, on-device configuration (via Android DataStore): how long the lock has been active today (resets daily) and whether Toddler-Proof Lock is enabled. This data never leaves the device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

Children's privacy

Touch Lock is intended to be downloaded, installed, and configured by an adult — it is a tool for parents and guardians, not an app directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone, children included, since we do not collect data at all.
